The Problem

The traditional credentialing system is fundamentally broken. Degrees have become commoditized signals, while the rapid pace of technological change renders rigid curricula obsolete before students even graduate.

Employers are drowning in resumes filled with unverified claims of "proficiency." There is a massive trust gap between what a candidate claims to know and what they can actually execute on day one. We are relying on proxies for competence instead of demanding proof.
